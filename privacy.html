LA PLAZA / FARAZ STUDIO

Privacy Policy

Policy version 2.0

1. Scope and publisher

Faraz Studio publishes La Plaza, a single-player strategy game that can be played offline. The Android app is free and supported by advertising. This policy describes the game, its local storage, and the advertising service built into the Android app. The Faraz Studio website, social networks, app stores, and any website hosting this policy are separate services.

2. Information collected by the game

La Plaza does not require an account or ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos, or payment information. The game has no analytics, no remote gameplay services, and no social tracking widgets. Faraz Studio does not receive your campaign progress or audio preferences. The Android app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob), described in section 3, which is the only third-party service that may collect information through the app.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

When your device is connected to the internet, the Android app may show a full-screen ad at a natural break, such as after a campaign ends. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob. Google may collect and process your IP address, the device's advertising ID, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language, and app version), approximate location derived from your IP address, ad interactions (impressions and clicks), and ad-related diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver, measure, and protect ads against fraud and, where permitted and you have consented, to personalize ads. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form before ads are requested, and a Privacy Choices button in Settings lets you change it later. You can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings. Learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. If you play offline, no ads are requested and the game remains fully playable.

4. Information stored on your device

The game stores campaign progress, a recovery checkpoint, random-generator state, pending game events, audio preferences, and briefing completion in the app's local WebView storage. The browser edition uses local site storage. These records let you resume play, recover a previous checkpoint, and retain your preferences. They are not used for advertising or profiling. On Android, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may also store your consent choice and ad-related identifiers on the device.

5. Network access and permissions

Game images, map geometry, fonts, icons, and audio are bundled locally. The Android app requests internet and network-state access and the advertising ID permission, which are used only by the advertising service. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or location permissions. The browser edition requires a connection to its host to load unless otherwise cached and does not show ads. Device platforms and app stores may process technical or diagnostic information independently of the game.

6. Optional external links

You may choose to open farazstudio.com or Faraz Studio's YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok pages. Those links open an external service, which may receive your IP address, browser information, account information, or cookies according to its own privacy policy. La Plaza does not embed their tracking widgets.

7. This policy on the web

Reading this policy from GitHub Pages or another web host creates a request to that host. The hosting provider may process IP addresses and technical request logs under its own privacy policy. This policy page itself contains no scripts, analytics, cookies, external fonts, or third-party embeds. Hosting this page does not make offline campaign records available to the host.

8. Audience and privacy choices

La Plaza is a fictional strategy game with mature themes and is not directed to children. Consult the current age rating on the platform where you obtain it. The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children, and its ads are not configured as child-directed. You can change advertising consent through Privacy Choices in Settings where offered, and control the advertising ID in your device settings. Rights concerning information processed by Google should be exercised with Google under its privacy policy and applicable law.

9. Retention, deletion, and security

Local records remain until replaced or removed. Starting a new campaign replaces the active match but may leave a recovery checkpoint and does not erase audio preferences or briefing completion. To remove all game records on Android, use the device's Settings to clear La Plaza's app storage. In a browser, clear site data for the game's origin. Faraz Studio does not provide a campaign cloud-sync service and cannot restore records it does not receive. Your operating system or backup service may separately back up local application data under its own settings. Keeping your device updated helps protect local records; no storage method can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes and contact

This policy will be revised if the game's data practices change. For publisher information, visit the Faraz Studio website at https://farazstudio.com or send privacy inquiries to farazstudio.dev@gmail.com. If you contact us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include, and use it to respond to and manage your request. Do not send campaign files or other personal information unless needed for your request.
